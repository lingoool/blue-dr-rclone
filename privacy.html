Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

blue-dr-rclone is a private Google OAuth client used only by its owner to back up their own computer to their own Google Drive with rclone and restic.

What it accesses

Only the drive.file permission: the files and folders this client creates in the owner's Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other Drive files.

What it does with the data

  • Backup data is encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. Google and anyone else see only encrypted files.
  • The login token is stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, used for advertising or sent to any server other than Google Drive.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used, including on this website.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the developer contact email shown on this app's Google sign-in screen.
